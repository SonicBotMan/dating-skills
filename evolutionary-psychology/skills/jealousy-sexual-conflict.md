# Jealousy & Sexual Conflict | 嫉妒与两性冲突

## Concept | 概念

Sexual jealousy represents an evolved psychological mechanism designed to prevent mate infidelity and protect reproductive investment. The form of jealousy differs by sex: men are more distressed by sexual infidelity (paternity threat), while women are more distressed by emotional infidelity (resource diversion). This chapter examines conflict between sexes arising from fundamentally different reproductive strategies.

性嫉妒是一种进化形成的心理机制，旨在防止配偶不忠并保护繁殖投资。嫉妒的形式因性别而异：男性对性不忠更痛苦（父子关系威胁），而女性对情感不忠更痛苦（资源转移）。本章探讨由根本上不同的繁殖策略引起的两性冲突。

## RIA++ Framework | RIA++框架

### Situation | 情境

**The Asymmetric Threats | 不对称威胁**:

```
Male Threat from Cuckoldry:
├── Lost paternity certainty
├── Resources invested in another's child
├── Zero reproductive payoff
└── Adaptations: Sexual jealousy mechanisms

Female Threat from Resource Diversion:
├── Lost resources for her and offspring
├── Partner's investment redirected to rival
├── No protection for her children
└── Adaptations: Emotional jealousy mechanisms
```

**The Fundamental Difference | 根本差异**:

| Infidelity Type | Male Response | Female Response |
|-----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Partner's Sexual Infidelity | More distressed | Less distressed |
| Partner's Emotional Infidelity | Less distressed | More distressed |

**Explanation | 解释**:
- Males: Sexual infidelity = possible cuckoldry = wasted investment
- Females: Emotional infidelity = resource diversion = lost protection

### Insight | 洞察

**The Sexual vs Emotional Infidelity Distinction | 性不忠vs情感不忠**:

Daly & Wilson (1988) found:
- 60% of men would be "extremely distressed" by sexual infidelity
- Only 13% of women would be

- 47% of women would be "extremely distressed" by emotional infidelity
- Only 22% of men would be

**Evolutionary Logic | 进化逻辑**:

> A man's sexual jealousy is calibrated to the unique adaptive problem: he can never be 100% certain of his paternity. His mate's sexual infidelity is the ultimate threat to his reproductive success.

男性的性嫉妒经过校准以应对独特适应性问题：他永远无法100%确定自己的父子关系。配偶的性不忠是对其繁殖成功的终极威胁。

> A woman's emotional jealousy is calibrated to the unique adaptive problem: her reproductive success depends on sustained resources. A mate's emotional attachment to a rival means those resources are no longer exclusively hers.

女性的情感嫉妒经过校准以应对独特适应性问题：她的繁殖成功依赖于持续的资源。配偶对竞争对手的情感依附意味着这些资源不再专属于她。

### Application | 应用

**Interpreting Relationship Conflicts | 解读关系冲突**:

1. **Male Jealousy Outbursts**:
   - Often triggered by perceived sexual threat
   - May involve mate-guarding behaviors
   - Not simply "insecurity" but evolved threat response

2. **Female Jealousy Outbursts**:
   - Often triggered by resource/threat perception
   - May involve rival derogation
   - Not simply "possessiveness" but resource protection

**Conflict Resolution | 冲突解决**:

Understanding sex differences allows better navigation:
- Male perspective: Address sexual fidelity concerns first
- Female perspective: Address emotional and resource commitment first

**Warning Signs | 警示信号**:

For male infidelity risk:
- Unexplained absences
- Guarded phone/passwords
- Sudden interest in appearance
- Changes in sexual routine

For female resource diversion risk:
- Emotional distancing
- Less interest in family activities
- Financial secrecy
- Comparisons to other women's lifestyles

---

### Evidence | 证据

**Cross-Cultural Studies | 跨文化研究**:

Buss et al. (1992) studied jealousy in 13 cultures:
- Male > Female sexual jealousy: 13/13 cultures
- Female > Male emotional jealousy: 11/13 cultures

This pattern is one of the most robust findings in evolutionary psychology.

**Physiological Responses | 生理反应**:

When presented with infidelity scenarios:
- Men show heightened cortisol response to sexual infidelity
- Women show heightened cortisol response to emotional infidelity
- Different autonomic nervous system activation patterns

## Summary | 总结

```
JEALOUSY AS ADAPTATION:

Male Calibration:
├── Primary threat: Sexual infidelity → Cuckoldry
├── Mechanism: Possessiveness, mate-guarding
├── Response: Confrontation, violence risk
└── Root: Paternity uncertainty

Female Calibration:
├── Primary threat: Emotional/Resource infidelity
├── Mechanism: Rival surveillance, derogation
├── Response: Resource securing, mate retention
└── Root: Resource dependency

KEY INSIGHT:
What appears "irrational" in modern relationships
reflects evolutionary pressures from an ancestral
environment where:
- Men couldn't prove paternity
- Women couldn't independently provide resources
```
