# Short-Term Mating Psychology | 短期择偶心理学

## Concept | 概念

Short-term mating strategies refer to casual sexual relationships without long-term commitment. Evolutionary theory predicts sex differences in short-term mating psychology due to asymmetric parental investment and different potential reproductive payoffs.

短期择偶策略指没有长期承诺的随意性关系。进化理论预测，由于亲代投资的不对称性和潜在繁殖收益的差异，短期择偶心理学存在性别差异。

## RIA++ Framework | RIA++框架

### Situation | 情境

**The Asymmetric Problem | 不对称问题**:

| Factor | Male | Female |
|--------|------|--------|
| Minimum investment | Few minutes | 9 months gestation + years nursing |
| Potential offspring from one encounter | 1+ children | 1 child max |
| Reproductive ceiling | Very high (100s) | ~12 maximum |
| Certainty of offspring | Cannot guarantee | 100% (after birth) |

**The Core Dilemma | 核心困境**:
```
Male: One night = potential child with no cost
Female: One night = potential 9 months + permanent child
```

Evolutionary mismatch: Modern contraception removes ancient risks but psychological mechanisms remain calibrated to ancestral environment.

---

### Task | 任务

**Male Short-Term Task | 男性短期任务**:
1. Maximize number of sexual partners
2. Minimize time-to-sex
3. Identify sexually accessible females
4. Avoid commitment signals
5. Reduce standard criteria

**Female Short-Term Task | 女性短期任务**:
1. Identify genetic quality superior to current partner
2. Extract resources from short-term encounter
3. Assess for potential mate-switching
4. Maintain reputation for future long-term options
5. Time estrus for optimal conception

### Action | 行动

**Male Short-Term Mechanisms | 男性短期机制**:

| Mechanism | Description | Evidence |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Partner Number Desire | Desire for 18+ lifetime partners vs women's 4-5 | Buss & Schmitt (1993) |
| Time-to-Sex Reduction | Willing after very short acquaintance | Accept at 1 week acquaintance |
| Standard Lowering | Relax 51 of 67 criteria | Especially for non-commitment cues |
| Commitment Avoidance | Reject commitment-seeking partners | -2.17 vs +2.17 importance |
| Visual Fantasy | 2x more sexual fantasies | Ellis & Symons |
| "Closing Time" Effect | Increased attraction at midnight | Gladue & Delaney |

**Female Short-Term Mechanisms | 女性短期机制**:

| Mechanism | Description | Evidence |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Ovulation Timing | Prefer masculine during fertile window | Johnston et al. |
| Extra-Pair Attraction | Around ovulation, seek good genes | Penton-Voak & Perrett |
| Resource Extraction | Seek gifts/resources during encounter | Buss & Schmitt |
| Mate Switching | Use affair to evaluate new partner | Greiling & Buss |
| Self-Resemblance | Prefer symmetrical partners | Gangestad & Thornhill |

### Insight | 洞察

**Why Women Engage in Short-Term Mating | 女性为何参与短期择偶**:

Research supports these hypotheses (ranked by evidence strength):

1. **Resources Hypothesis | 资源假设**: Access to resources, gifts, protection
2. **Good Genes Hypothesis | 好基因假设**: Obtain superior genetic material
3. **Mate-Switching Hypothesis | 更换配偶假设**: Evaluate alternatives, exit bad relationships
4. **Sexual Skills Hypothesis | 性技巧假设**: Learn seduction techniques
5. **Mate-Guarding Hypothesis | 操纵配偶假设**: Make jealous partner more attentive

**The Ovulation Effect | 排卵期效应**:

```
Not fertile → Prefer provider traits
Fertile window → Prefer genetic quality traits (masculinity, symmetry)
```

This dual-strategy reflects ancestral pressure to secure both resources (long-term) and good genes (short-term, especially during peak fertility).

### Application | 应用

**Warning Signs | 警示信号**:

For women detecting men's short-term orientation:
- Disproportionate emphasis on physical appearance
- Rapid escalation to sexual topics
- Avoids commitment language
- Generic "dating" vs relationship talk
- Minimal investment in getting-to-know-you
- "Just seeing what's out there"

For men detecting women's short-term orientation:
- High openness to sex without relationship
- Focus on physical attractiveness of partners
- Lower commitment requirements
- History of short relationships
- High partner count

**Strategy Calibration | 策略校准**:

| Personal Factor | Male Strategy | Female Strategy |
|----------------|---------------|----------------|
| High mate value | More short-term options | Higher standards |
| Low mate value | More selective | More practical standards |
| High self-esteem | Less casual sex | More selective |
| Low self-esteem | More short-term | More vulnerable to exploitation |

### Evidence | 证据

**Physiological Evidence | 生理学证据**:

1. **Testes Size | 睾丸大小**:
   - Human: 0.079% of body weight
   - Gorilla: 0.018% (monogamous, low competition)
   - Chimpanzee: 0.269% (promiscuous, high competition)
   - Human is intermediate → moderate sperm competition

2. **Sperm Count Adaptation | 精子数量适应**:
   - Increases after partner separation
   - Suggests adaptation to perceived infidelity risk

**Behavioral Evidence | 行为证据**:

- 40-50% of married individuals have extramarital sex (Kinsey)
- 75% of males would accept casual sex offer (vs 0% females in Clarke & Hatfield study)
- Women's affair timing correlated with ovulation (Baker & Bellis)

### Example | 示例

**Scenario: College Party | 场景：大学派对**

At midnight, a man evaluates women differently than earlier:
- Earlier: Looking for long-term qualities
- Near closing: More likely to approach less attractive women
- Near closing: More willing to accept sexual offers

This "closing time effect" is not intoxication but evolved mechanism calibrated to decreasing opportunity cost.

## Summary | 总结

```
Male Short-Term Psychology:
├── Desire: Maximize partner number
├── Time: Minimize to sex
├── Standards: Lower significantly  
├── Commitment: Avoid
└── Evidence: Fantasy, affairs, prostitution patronage

Female Short-Term Psychology:
├── Desire: Resources OR good genes
├── Timing: Estrus-hidden → strategic
├── Standards: Remains selective on key traits
├── Reputation: Must protect future options
└── Evidence: Affairs, ovulation timing, affair satisfaction

Key Insight:
Short-term and long-term strategies coexist in both sexes,
with context and individual differences determining which activates.
```
