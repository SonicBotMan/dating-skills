# Mate Selection Strategy | 择偶策略

## Concept | 概念

The evolved psychological mechanisms humans use to evaluate and select long-term mates. Based on Trivers' Parental Investment Theory and Buss' cross-cultural research across 37 cultures.

人类评估和选择长期配偶的进化心理机制。基于Trivers的亲代投资理论和Buss跨越37种文化的跨文化研究。

---

## RIA++ Framework | RIA++框架

### Situation | 情境

When evaluating a potential long-term partner, both sexes face adaptive problems but with different selection criteria due to differences in parental investment and reproductive biology.

评估潜在长期配偶时，两性都面临适应性问题，但由于亲代投资和生殖生物学差异，择偶标准不同。

**Female Adaptive Problem | 女性适应性问题**:
- Resource acquisition for offspring survival
- Need reliable, long-term commitment
- Ensure male will provide parental investment
- Protect offspring from other males

**Male Adaptive Problem | 男性适应性问题**:
- Assess female fertility/reproductive value
- Ensure paternity certainty
- Evaluate female's commitment likelihood
- Assess offspring survival capability

---

### Task | 任务

**For Females | 女性任务**:
1. Evaluate resource acquisition capability (current + future)
2. Assess commitment reliability and duration
3. Judge parental investment willingness
4. Evaluate physical protection ability
5. Check health/genetic quality signals

**For Males | 男性任务**:
1. Assess youth and fertility signals
2. Evaluate sexual fidelity/likelihood
3. Judge commitment to offspring
4. Check health indicators
5. Evaluate reproductive value trajectory

| Signal Category | What to Observe | Reliability |
|----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Economic Resources | Income, education, occupation, assets | Current status |
| Resource Trajectory | Ambition, work ethic, career path | Future potential |
| Commitment Signals | Love behaviors, sacrifice, dedication | Direct observation |

**Male Mate Evaluation Matrix | 男性择偶评估矩阵**:

|----------------|-----------------|-------------|

| Virginity/Fidelity | Sexual history, relationship history | Probability signals |
| Commitment Signals | Self-sacrifice, dedication behaviors | Direct observation |
| Parental Instinct | Interaction with children, nurturing | Behavioral test |

### Insight | 洞察

**Key Evolutionary Insight | 关键进化洞察**:

> Female mate preferences are not arbitrary preferences but evolved solutions to the "resource provisioning problem" - women who successfully solved this problem left more offspring.

女性的择偶偏好不是任意偏好，而是解决"资源供给问题"的进化方案——成功解决此问题的女性留下了更多后代。

**The Commitment Problem | 承诺问题**:
```
短期信号 → 易伪装 → 不可靠
长期行为 → 难伪装 → 可靠

评估方法: 时间考验 + 声誉核实 + 行为观察
```

**Paternity Uncertainty Effect | 父子关系不确定效应**:
- Males cannot be 100% certain of paternity
- This reduces investment incentive
- Female signals of fidelity reduce this uncertainty
- Physical resemblance claims (by mother) also affect paternal investment

---

### Application | 应用

**For Mate Selection | 择偶应用**:

1. **Resource Assessment | 资源评估**:
   - Current economic status
   - Education and career trajectory
   - Work ethic and ambition (better predictor than current income)
   - Actual resource control vs. potential

2. **Commitment Verification | 承诺验证**:
   - Time-tested behaviors over words
   - Reputation from social network
   - Willingness to sacrifice other opportunities
   - Investment patterns (time, money, energy)

3. **Parental Instinct Test | 亲本本能测试**:
   - Observe genuine interaction with children
   - Response to children's distress
   - Nurturing behaviors unprompted
   - La Cerra (1994): Women rate men higher who interact positively with children

4. **Context Sensitivity | 背景敏感性**:
   - Long-term vs short-term contexts require different standards
   - Ovulatory cycle affects preferences (higher testosterone preference near ovulation)
   - Personal mate value affects standards (higher value → higher standards)

---

### Calculation | 计算

**Female Mate Value Formula | 女性配偶价值公式**:
```
MV = f(youth, health, fertility_markers, symmetry)
```
Higher MV → More options → Higher standards

**Male Mate Value Formula | 男性配偶价值公式**:
```
MV = f(resources, status, commitment_ability, protection_ability, genetic_quality)
```
Higher MV → More options → More short-term opportunities

### Example | 示例

**Scenario: Online Dating Profile Evaluation | 场景：在线 dating 个人档案评估**

Female evaluating male profile:
1. Check education/career (resource proxy)
2. Photos showing activities (status signals)
3. Self-description keywords: ambition, family-oriented
4. "What I can offer" section vs "what I want"
5. Question: Does he show commitment capacity or just seeking short-term?

Red flags for women:
- Only shows physical attractiveness
- No mention of career or goals
- Heavy emphasis on physical attributes wanted
- Short-term relationship indicators

Red flags for men:
- Emphasis on youth/beauty only
- No mention of relationship goals
- Professional "model" photos
- Ambiguous about commitment

---

### Warning | 警示

**EvolutionaryMismatch Alert | 进化失配警告**:

Modern environments create mismatches:
- Online photos don't reveal behavior
- Wealth displays may be borrowed/decorative
- Commitment signals in modern contexts different from ancestral

**Cross-Cultural Limits | 跨文化限制**:

While core preferences universal (resources for women, youth for men), expression and relative importance vary by:
- Economic conditions
- Mate availability
- Cultural marriage systems
- Female economic independence

## Summary | 总结

```
Female Priority Stack:
1. Resource commitment reliability
2. Parental investment willingness  
3. Physical protection capability
4. Genetic quality signals

Male Priority Stack:
1. Youth/fertility indicators
2. Paternity certainty signals
3. Commitment likelihood
4. Health/genetic quality

Key Principle:
Both sexes evolved context-sensitive mechanisms
calibrated by their own mate value and
the mate pool characteristics.
```
